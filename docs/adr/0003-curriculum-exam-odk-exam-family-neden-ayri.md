# 0003 — CurriculumExam and OdkExamFamily remain separate

Status: Proposed

`CurriculumExam` classifies curriculum versions and includes YDT. `OdkExamFamily` governs ODK exam series/exams and currently supports LGS, TYT, and AYT only.

Decision: keep separate so curriculum coverage can expand independently of the commercial exam engine. Consolidation would make unsupported ODK families representable without the corresponding product behavior.
